Safety Guidelines

Your safety is our top priority. At Get Local Friends, we are committed to fostering a safe and respectful community. While we implement tools and strategies to support user well-being, you are ultimately responsible for your own safety while interacting with others online or in person. By using this platform, you acknowledge and accept these responsibilities.

1. General Safety Practices

Always meet in public places for initial in-person interactions. Do not disclose sensitive personal information until trust is fully established. Use caution when sharing your location, contact information, or financial details. Inform a trusted friend or family member of your plans, especially when meeting someone new. Trust your instincts—if something feels off, prioritize your safety and disengage.

2. Reporting and Blocking

We provide tools to report abusive or suspicious behavior and to block users who make you feel uncomfortable. If you witness or experience harassment, threats, fraud, or any violation of our community standards, please report it immediately.

3. No Liability Disclaimer

While we strive to protect users through proactive monitoring and responsive support, Get Local Friends is not responsible for the actions, conduct, or behavior of any user on or off the platform. You assume all risks associated with meeting others, whether online or in person, and agree to indemnify and hold harmless the platform and its operators from any resulting claims or damages.

4. Emergencies

In an emergency or if you believe you are in immediate danger, contact local authorities right away. Our team can assist with follow-up support after safety has been secured.

5. Community Culture

Safety is a shared responsibility. We encourage users to be respectful, inclusive, and supportive of one another. Upholding these values is essential to building genuine, long-lasting friendships through our platform.

6. Contact Us

For safety-related questions, reports, or follow-up support, please contact us at getlocalfriends@gmail.com.
